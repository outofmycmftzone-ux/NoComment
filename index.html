The
rules.

  1. Players take turns choosing a deck and drawing one card.
  2. Complete the card, or choose NO COMMENT and draw a punishment.
  3. Each player gets one Double Down token per game. Spend it to draw a second card from the same deck. Complete both to earn a reward.
  4. After a successful Double Down, choose one of two randomly drawn rewards.
  5. Each player can hold only one unused reward at a time. Use it before attempting another Double Down.
  6. Cards do not repeat until that deck is exhausted.
  7. Anyone can pass on unwanted contact, intimate disclosure or unsafe activity.
  8. Drinking is optional. Never pressure anyone to consume alcohol.
