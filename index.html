The
rules.

  1. Players take turns choosing a deck and drawing one card.
  2. Each card starts at a randomly selected intensity: Level 2 or Level 3.
  3. Complete the card, or choose NO COMMENT and draw a punishment.
  4. Each player gets one Double Down token per game. Spend it to draw a second card from the same deck or raise your current card one intensity level.
  5. Cards do not repeat until that deck is exhausted. An intensity upgrade replaces the current card; it is not an extra draw.
  6. Anyone can pass on unwanted contact, intimate disclosure or unsafe activity.
  7. Drinking is optional. Never pressure anyone to consume alcohol.
